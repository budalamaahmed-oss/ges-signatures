The challenge

What organisations in this position face

  • Misaligned technology and business strategy, with investments not mapping directly to enterprise objectives.
  • Complex, inefficient processes and no single view of enterprise risk across business units.
  • Resistance to change without a structured organisational change-management approach.

The approach

People and process first, then the platform

GES treated risk management as an organisational capability rather than a document. The engagement combined a risk framework aligned with ISO 31000 with the governance structures, ownership and reporting rhythm needed to keep the register current after handover.

What GES delivered

Scope of the engagement

  • An enterprise risk management framework aligned with ISO 31000.
  • Risk identification, assessment and treatment processes with clear ownership.
  • Governance structures and reporting for executive decision-making.
  • Change management to embed the framework across the group.
