# Image brief — Global GES website

Every image follows the Assets plan in `design.md`: GES consultants with a client's leadership in a warm, modern, architectural interior. Stone, glass, soft daylight, ivory tones. Architecture is the setting, never the subject. No glowing screens, no neon, no circuit boards, no pointing-at-laptops stock.

## Shared style line (append to every prompt)

> Soft diffused daylight from large windows, early morning, no direct sun. Warm ivory and pale stone palette with charcoal-navy shadows, one quiet note of deep blue in clothing or shadow, never bright blue. Muted, low-contrast, editorial documentary finish, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural skin tones, slight warm haze. No text, no watermark, no logos, no signage, no warped hands or faces.

## Prompts

**1 · Hero (optional regeneration), 4:5 vertical, 1600×2000.** Four professionals working as one team around a large pale-oak table in a Riyadh corporate meeting room: a GES consultant in a charcoal suit standing and pointing at a printed enterprise-architecture diagram, a client executive in a white thobe and ghutra seated and reading it, a woman in a navy abaya and headscarf annotating a process map, a second consultant leaning in. A travertine stone wall behind them, floor-to-ceiling glass to one side, a single closed laptop and paper documents on the table, no screens on. Calm, collaborative, unposed, mid-conversation. Composition: table in the lower third, people centred, stone wall filling the upper half for the headline to sit beside. Shot on a 35mm lens at eye level. [shared style line]

**2 · Banking reference, 3:2 horizontal, 1800×1200.** The atrium of a modern Saudi bank headquarters: pale limestone columns, a wide travertine floor, a mashrabiya-patterned screen casting soft geometric daylight across the floor, two employees in Saudi business attire walking in the middle distance, slightly out of focus. Quiet, orderly, early in the working day. Composition: strong verticals, people small in frame, the light pattern as the visual event. Shot on a 35mm lens, eye level. [shared style line]

**3 · Healthcare reference, 3:2 horizontal, 1800×1200.** The covered entrance walkway of a modern hospital campus in Riyadh at early morning: pale sandstone and glass facade, slender columns, a few date palms, a nurse and a doctor walking away from camera at a distance, long soft shadows on a pale stone floor, gentle haze. Clean, calm, unhurried. Composition: walkway leading diagonally into the frame, sky pale and nearly white. Shot on a 35mm lens, eye level. [shared style line]

**4 · Transport reference, 3:2 horizontal, 1800×1200.** New transport infrastructure on the Omani coast at dawn: a highway interchange with pale concrete bridges curving past rocky ochre hills, a station canopy in the middle distance, sparse traffic, a pale blue-grey sky. Elevated viewpoint from a hillside, calm and orderly. Composition: the curve of the road as the line of the image, horizon in the upper third. Shot on a 35mm lens, slightly elevated. [shared style line]
