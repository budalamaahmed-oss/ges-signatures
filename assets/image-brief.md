# Image brief — Global GES website

Every image follows the Assets plan in `design.md`: a GES team with a client's leadership in a warm, modern, architectural interior. Stone, glass, soft daylight, ivory tones. Architecture is the setting, never the subject. No glowing screens, no neon, no circuit boards, no pointing-at-laptops stock.

Shared style suffix for every prompt:
> Warm ivory and stone palette, soft diffused daylight, muted warm colour grade, low contrast, 35mm lens, natural skin tones, no text, no logos, no signage.

## Prompts

**Hero (if regenerating).** Editorial documentary photograph, vertical. A consulting team of four professionals working with a client's leadership in a modern Gulf corporate meeting room: two men in dark suits, one man in a white thobe and ghutra, one woman in a navy abaya and headscarf, gathered around a large pale oak table reviewing printed process diagrams and an enterprise architecture plan, one person pointing at the paper. Travertine stone wall, floor-to-ceiling glass, soft morning daylight, a single closed laptop. Calm, collaborative, unposed.

**Reference 1 · Banking.** Editorial architectural photograph, horizontal. Interior atrium of a modern bank headquarters in Riyadh: pale limestone columns, a wide travertine floor, a mashrabiya-patterned screen casting soft geometric daylight, two employees in Saudi business attire walking in the middle distance, slightly out of focus. Quiet and orderly.

**Reference 2 · Healthcare.** Editorial architectural photograph, horizontal. Exterior of a modern hospital campus in Saudi Arabia at early morning: pale sandstone and glass facade, a covered walkway with slender columns, a few date palms, a nurse and a doctor walking at a distance, long soft shadows. Gentle haze, calm and clean.

**Reference 3 · Transport.** Editorial documentary photograph, horizontal. Modern transport infrastructure in Oman at dawn: a coastal highway interchange with pale concrete bridges curving past rocky hills, a station canopy in the middle distance, sparse traffic, soft warm morning light, pale blue sky. Elevated viewpoint, calm and orderly.

## Notes

- Do not depict real named institutions. The references are sector scenes, labelled by sector on the page.
- Logos are excluded from this brief: clients and partners use their real logo files.
- The blue neon data-centre image from the current site is not to be used anywhere.
