Privacy

Privacy notice.

How Global GES handles the information you share through this website.

Draft for review by GES legal and compliance before publication. The wording below is a starting point, not approved policy.

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form or email us, we receive the details you provide: your name, company, business email, phone number, country, area of interest and message. The website itself sets no tracking cookies.

Why we collect it

To respond to your enquiry, to connect you with the relevant GES expert, and to follow up on a requirement, project or partnership you have asked about. We do not sell or share your details with third parties for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are retained for as long as needed to respond and, where a business relationship follows, for the duration of that relationship and any period required by law.

Your rights

You may ask what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to legal and contractual obligations. Write to info@globalges.net.

Regulatory basis

Global GES operates in Saudi Arabia and across the region and handles personal data in line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and applicable local regulation in each jurisdiction where it operates.
