What GES does

Six responsibilities. One accountable partner.

Technology selection is only the start. GES stays responsible for the solution across the lifecycle, so the client is never left between a vendor and an integrator.

  1. 01

    Selection

    Match the requirement to the right platform on the client's criteria, not the vendor's.

  2. 02

    Consulting

    Design the processes, governance and architecture the technology has to serve.

  3. 03

    Implementation

    Deploy, configure and test against the agreed scope and the client's environment.

  4. 04

    Integration

    Connect the solution to identity, data, enterprise platforms and existing operations.

  5. 05

    Operation

    Run and monitor the environment, on-site or from the GES operations centre.

  6. 06

    Support

    Keep it working with a 24/7 support centre, maintenance and continuous improvement.

How technology is selected

Chosen on the client's criteria, not the catalogue's.

  1. 01

    Cost

    Total cost across licensing, implementation, integration and operation, not the headline price.

  2. 02

    References

    Proven deployments in comparable organisations, sectors and regulatory environments.

  3. 03

    Performance

    Measured against the client's real workloads, service levels and growth.

  4. 04

    Usability

    Adoption by the people who will use it every day, with the training to make it stick.

  5. 05

    Integration

    Fit with identity, data, enterprise platforms and the existing operating environment.
