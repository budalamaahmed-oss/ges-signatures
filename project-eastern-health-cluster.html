The challenge

What organisations in this position face

  • Data silos and fragmented assets across clinical, administrative and operational systems, preventing a single source of truth and cross-departmental reporting.
  • Compliance and governance mandates under SADAIA NDMO and DAMA DMBOK, requiring strict data privacy and stewardship controls.
  • Poor data quality and unstandardised formats leading to inaccurate analytics and delayed decision-making.

The approach

People and process first, then the platform

GES assessed the current data landscape before defining the office: ownership, stewardship, policies and the operating rhythm that would keep them alive. The Data Management Office was designed as a function with people and processes first, and platforms second, so that governance would survive the project team.

What GES delivered

Scope of the engagement

  • Data governance policies, stewardship roles and a framework aligned with SADAIA NDMO and DAMA DMBOK.
  • Data architecture and warehousing direction: modelling, ETL pipelines and secure storage.
  • Master data and quality control: cleansing, deduplication, identity resolution and quality monitoring.
  • BI and analytics foundations: dashboards, enterprise reporting and predictive analytics.
