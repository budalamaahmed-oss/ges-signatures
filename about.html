About Experts Gate

Connecting expertise, technology and opportunity across the Middle East & Africa.

Experts Gate is the business advisory, technology and professional-development arm of Global Experts Services (GES). We support government entities and private-sector organizations in strengthening capabilities, improving performance and accelerating sustainable growth.

HeritagePresenceEcosystemPeople

Heritage

One partner able to connect the boardroom, the architecture and the operating environment.

Through an integrated portfolio of consultancy, technology solutions and training, Experts Gate connects clients with specialized expertise, proven methodologies and trusted international partners, delivering solutions tailored to the operational and strategic priorities of organizations throughout the Middle East and Africa.

Partnership architecture extends delivery without loss of accountability. That distinction separates Experts Gate from a reseller or a systems integrator: every engagement is designed from Diagnose and measured at Sustain by the same accountable team.

Experts Gate is part of Global Experts Services (GES), an IT services and consulting group serving banks, ministries, universities and government entities across the Gulf and beyond.
